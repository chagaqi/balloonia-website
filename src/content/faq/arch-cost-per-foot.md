question: How much does a balloon arch cost per foot?

A per-foot rate is the most common way people try to price balloon work, and it is the number most likely to be wrong by the time the install happens. Two ten-foot arches can differ by several hundred dollars depending on balloon density, how many colours are in the blend, and whether the shape needs a frame underneath it. A loose organic arch across a doorway uses a fraction of the balloons that a packed full-coverage arch of the same width does. What we can tell you honestly is where finished arches land in London Ontario. Small organic arches for a photo spot or classroom start around $299. Standard doorway and ceremony arches run $499 to $999. Large statement arches for venues, grand openings, and full ceremony backdrops reach $1,200 and up when they need structural framing. If you have a measurement and a reference photo, that is enough for Brenda to quote the real number within 24 hours, which beats a per-foot estimate that changes once she sees the space.
