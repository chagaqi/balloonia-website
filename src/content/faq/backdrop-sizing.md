---
question: What size should a balloon backdrop be for photos?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'walls', 'photo-booth']
order: 17
---

For a backdrop that people stand in front of one or two at a time, eight feet wide by eight feet tall is the size that works. That gives a photographer room to frame a couple from the knees up without the edge of the wall creeping into the shot. For group photos, and this is where most people undersize, you want ten to twelve feet wide. A row of six guests takes more space than it looks like it will, and a backdrop that ends behind someone's shoulder is the thing you notice in every photo afterward. Height matters more than width for anyone standing. Anything under seven feet tall starts showing the ceiling or the wall behind it once a camera is at eye level. If your venue in London has a low ceiling, we build wider and lower and angle it instead. Send us the dimensions of the wall you have in mind, or a photo of the corner you are thinking about, and we will tell you what actually fits before you commit to a size.
