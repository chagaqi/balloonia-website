---
question: How long do helium balloons last?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'birthdays', 'graduations']
order: 14
---

Latex helium balloons float for roughly eight to twelve hours untreated, which covers a single event day but not much past it. Treated with a sealant on the inside, the same balloon holds for two to three days. Foil and mylar balloons, including number and letter balloons, hold helium far longer and typically float for three to five days, often up to two weeks indoors. Temperature is the variable people underestimate. A balloon that moves from a warm car into an air conditioned hall will visibly shrink, then recover once it warms back up. Cold shortens float time noticeably. This is also why most of our large installations in London are air-filled rather than helium. Air-filled arches, garlands, and walls hold their shape for days or weeks instead of hours, which is why they suit weddings, corporate events, and anything set up the night before. If your event runs across multiple days, tell us at booking and we will build it in the medium that lasts.
