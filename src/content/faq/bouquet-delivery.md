Yes, we deliver bouquets across London and the surrounding area, including St. Thomas, Strathroy, Ingersoll, and Woodstock. Bouquets are the one thing we make that can go out same day when the calendar allows, so if you are ordering for a birthday, a hospital visit, a new baby, or an office desk, message us and we will tell you honestly whether today is possible. A few practical notes. Helium bouquets float for roughly eight to twelve hours in latex and several days in foil, so timing the delivery close to when it needs to look its best matters more than ordering early. Balloons do not travel well in a hot car, so we deliver them ourselves rather than handing them to a courier. For deliveries to a workplace or a hospital, check that the recipient is allowed balloons at all, since some units in London hospitals do not permit latex. Order bouquets directly from the shop, or message us if you want a custom colour combination that is not listed.
