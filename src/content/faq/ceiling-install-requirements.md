---
question: What does a ceiling balloon installation need from the venue?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'ceiling', 'corporate', 'weddings']
order: 18
---

Three things, and all three are worth confirming before you book rather than on the day. First, an attachment point. Most ceiling work in London venues hangs from existing rigging points, beams, light bars, or drop ceiling grid. Some venues allow fishing line and clips, some allow nothing at all on the ceiling, and a few have rigging that only their own staff may touch. Second, height and access. Anything above about twelve feet needs a ladder or lift, and the venue has to let us bring one in and have floor space clear to set it up. Third, a setup window with the room empty. Ceiling work is the one install type that cannot happen around guests or around another vendor setting tables underneath. We usually ask for the room two to three hours ahead. Brenda confirms all of this directly with the venue coordinator, so you are not relaying answers between two vendors the week of your event. If you tell us the venue name when you enquire, we will often already know their rules.
