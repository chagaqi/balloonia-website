question: How many balloon centerpieces do I need?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'centerpieces', 'showers', 'corporate']
order: 19
---

One per guest table is the standard, and for most events in London that lands between six and twelve. Before you order that many, it is worth knowing that not every table needs the same thing. A common approach is a full centerpiece on the head table, the gift table, and the cake or dessert table, then something smaller and lower on the guest tables so people can see each other across them. Height is the detail that gets missed. Anything above about fourteen inches on a dining table blocks sightlines and guests end up moving it themselves, which is why we build guest table pieces low and wide and save the tall ones for tables nobody sits at. For corporate events and galas where tables are larger and rounds seat ten, we usually recommend fewer, larger pieces rather than one per table. Send us your table count and layout and we will tell you where the pieces actually earn their spot rather than quoting one for every surface.
