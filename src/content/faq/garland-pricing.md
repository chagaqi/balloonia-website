Balloon garland pricing in London Ontario comes down to length, colour count, and what we are attaching it to. A small accent garland for a table run, a backdrop corner, or a dessert display starts around $160. A standard garland for a shower, birthday, or office event, usually eight to twelve feet, runs $200 to $500. Large organic garlands that wrap a staircase, span a head table, or climb a full feature wall land between $700 and $1,200. Adding a second or third colour raises the price because every colour is a separate inflation batch and a separate sort before Brenda ever starts building. Attaching to brick, glass, or anything a venue will not let us drill into also changes the method and the cost. Every garland is built fresh for the booking rather than pulled from stock, so the quote reflects your actual palette and space. Send us the wall or the table you want covered, your colours, and your date, and we will come back with a real number within 24 hours.
