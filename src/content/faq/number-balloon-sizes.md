---
question: How tall are balloon columns and number balloons?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'columns', 'birthdays', 'graduations']
order: 20
---

Standard freestanding balloon columns run six to seven feet tall, which puts the top just above head height so they read from across a room. Number and letter balloons come in a few sizes. The common large foil numbers are about three feet tall and work mounted on a column base or floating in a cluster. Oversized mosaic numbers, the kind you fill with balloons and stand on the floor, are four feet tall and hold their own as a photo backdrop on their own. For a milestone birthday or a Class of 2026 grad setup in London, a pair of numbers on column bases flanking an entrance or a cake table is the setup we build most. Columns are freestanding and weighted, so they do not need to attach to anything, which makes them the easiest option for venues with rules about walls and ceilings. Outdoors they need extra weight or an anchor because wind moves them. Tell us the ceiling height and where you want them standing and we will size accordingly.
