---
question: How long does a balloon garland last outdoors?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'garlands', 'weddings', 'corporate']
order: 15
---

An air-filled garland installed outdoors in London holds well for a single event day and often several days in mild weather. Direct summer sun is the thing that shortens it. Sunlight causes latex to oxidize, which shows up as a chalky matte finish within a few hours, and heat expands the air inside enough that balloons in full sun can pop. We build for this when we know about it. Underinflating slightly gives the balloons room to expand, and positioning the install in shade or on a north-facing wall makes a visible difference. For outdoor weddings and grand openings we schedule setup as close to the start time as the venue allows rather than the night before. Foil and chrome finishes hold up better in sun than standard latex. Wind matters more than most people expect, so anything freestanding outdoors gets weighted or anchored. Tell us the install is outdoors when you book and we will pick materials and a setup window that survive the conditions rather than fighting them.
