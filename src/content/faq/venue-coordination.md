---
question: Do you work directly with venues and event planners?
pages: ['faq', 'services', 'corporate', 'weddings']
order: 16
---

Yes, and it is a large part of how we work. Brenda coordinates load-in directly with venue staff so the client is not relaying messages between two vendors on the week of the event. That means confirming access times, elevator and dock availability, what the venue allows us to attach to, and when the room is actually free before the event starts. Most venues in and around London have rules about adhesives, drilling, ceiling rigging, and how late a teardown can run, and getting those confirmed early is what keeps setup from becoming a problem on the day. For event planners and venue coordinators booking on behalf of a client, we can send a package overview, hold a date while a proposal is approved, and invoice the planner or the client directly. For recurring bookings such as monthly corporate events or a venue that wants a standing seasonal install, we can set up a standing arrangement so pricing and scheduling stay predictable. Send your venue and date and we will handle the coordination from there.
