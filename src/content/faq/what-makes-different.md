pages: ['faq', 'services', 'about']

Every install is built from scratch for your event. No catalog arches, no recycled props from a previous booking. Brenda starts with your colours, your venue dimensions, and what you actually want the space to feel like, then builds to that. She also delivers and installs on site and coordinates load-in directly with the venue, so your event day has one fewer thing to manage. Because each build is made to order, the quote reflects your actual palette and space rather than a fixed package. If you want to see the standard of work before committing, the portfolio shows real installs we have delivered around London and the surrounding area, and there is no cost to ask for a quote.
